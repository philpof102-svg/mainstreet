## 6:00–9:30 — The story that makes you credible

Tell exactly one, and tell the failure first. **This is the strongest 3 minutes you have — do not skip it
to fit more numbers in.**

"Our settlement indexer was frozen for six weeks and we didn't know. When we dug: the scheduler killed
the job at a 10-minute timeout while one chunk needs eleven minutes of RPC work, so no chunk ever
finished. And the child process's stderr was being discarded — 612 consecutive failures printed one
cheerful 'done' line. We had also been measuring our own staleness from the wrong end and reporting 70
hours when the truth was 244.

We fixed it yesterday. It's back at chain head. But the part I'd want you to notice is that the API now
publishes its own last-run outcome on a public endpoint — so the next freeze is visible to you, not just
to me. **A reputation oracle that hides its own outage has no business scoring anyone else.**"

- If they only remember one thing about you, this is the right thing.
- It also pre-empts the obvious objection ("how do we know your data is fresh?") by answering it before
  they ask.

**Second story, only if there's room** — shipped yesterday: we detect EIP-7702 delegated accounts.
An EOA that executes someone else's code, repointable by the owner at any time. We name the delegate
and vet it, because that's who you're actually paying. Standard wallet scoring answers the wrong
question there.

## 9:30–12:00 — What you want from them

Rank these. Ask for **one**, name the others as "later".

1. **Distribution.** Get MainStreet surfaced where agents already look — Base App / Coinbase Wallet agent
   profiles, the x402 Bazaar, ecosystem pages. "I'll do the PR, I need the door."
2. **A design partner.** One orchestrator or wallet team that routes payments and will tell me where the
   verdict is wrong. Traction is the gap, not features.
3. **Ecosystem Fund.** The application is written and honest — it discloses the six-week outage in the
   body, not a footnote. Ask what would make it competitive rather than whether it's good.

**Concrete asks, if they want something small to say yes to:**
- Intro to whoever owns agent discovery surfaces.
- A pointer on whether ERC-8004 + x402 reputation is a direction they want to see pushed, or a dead end
  in their view. That answer changes my roadmap and costs them nothing.
